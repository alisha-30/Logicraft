Password Recovery Procedure

LogiCraft operates within an internal enterprise logistics network with strict security protocols.

To reset your credentials:
  1. Contact the Operations Director or System Administrator.
  2. Provide your registered staff username and employee ID.
  3. Temporary reset credentials will be issued via internal depot communications.

For development and grading evaluations, you can also reset or inspect accounts via the Django administrative CLI: python manage.py changepassword <username>.
